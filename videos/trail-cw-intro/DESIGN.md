## Style Prompt

A conceptual promo opener for Trail CLI as a terminal-native gameplay Agent for Honkai: Star Rail. The film should feel closer to a product concept trailer than a terminal recording: large aligned Chinese copy carries the idea, abstract motion carries energy, and short terminal/gameplay glimpses serve as evidence. The viewer should understand the promise in seconds: Trail is a general CLI that gives Agents start/read/OCR/input capability for the game, while Currency Wars is the heavily optimized complex scenario showcased in this opener.

## Colors

- Background: `#050608`, almost black.
- Foreground: `#F4F1E8`, warm white for primary copy.
- Muted Text: `#9AA4A0`, restrained gray for small labels.
- Accent Primary: `#65F5D3`, cyan-teal for Agent state and verification.
- Accent Secondary: `#F6C95A`, warm gold for economy/action moments.
- Alert Accent: `#FF6F91`, used sparingly for risk/unknown state.

## Typography

- Large concept copy: `"Noto Sans SC", "Arial", sans-serif`, heavy, aligned to a consistent left rail.
- Terminal text: `"Cascadia Mono", "Consolas", monospace`, used only inside small terminal glimpses.
- Do not hide key messaging in terminal microcopy. Anything important to normal players must be large, readable, and spatially aligned.

## Motion

- Use seven short conceptual beats rather than one long terminal execution view.
- Large copy enters and exits as kinetic typography with scale, wipe, and slide motion.
- Abstract dots, route lines, cards, role icons, and equipment icons should move every second.
- Terminal/TUI appears in short bursts as a source of Agent state, not as the main picture for the whole video.
- Game screenshots are small observation chips, never full-screen or dominant.
- No floating corner words without a layout anchor. Large words must align to the same text rail or centered final lockup.
- Every rendered version must be reviewed as a continuous 6fps montage. Adjacent tiles should show frequent composition changes.

## Reference Takeaway

- OpenAI / ChatGPT references: high negative space, abstract dots/lines, large sparse copy.
- Gemini-style references: idea-to-result rather than mechanism-to-feature.
- Copilot Workspace reference: goal -> plan -> result, but without implying Trail owns a GUI workspace.
- Codex CLI / OpenCode / Oh-My-Pi: terminal is the honest Agent host, used as a clean status glimpse.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not turn the video into a terminal execution recording.
- Do not put the main message inside tiny terminal text.
- Do not use a three-column fake GUI dashboard.
- Do not fill the frame with game screenshots.
- Do not place big words in arbitrary corners.
- Do not explain CLI internals, protocol lines, YAML, debug events, or command mechanics.
- Do not imply Trail only supports Currency Wars. Currency Wars is the featured optimized scene, not the whole product boundary.
- Do not use generic AI/product filler like "state to decision" as the main message when accurate player language exists. Prefer terms from the active skills: 标准博弈、超频博弈、职级、投资环境、投资策略、攻略、普通备战、商店、手牌、装备、羁绊、金币、人口、出战、结算.
- Do not let decorative rings, dots, or lines sit under same-color headline strokes. In light sections, large dark decorations must stay away from the primary text block.
